1. WHAT WE ARE BUILDING
================================================================================
A single-page, premium-cute, animated birthday surprise website for Sneha.
It is NOT a boring static greeting — it is a full 16-scene interactive journey:

 - Starts with a FAKE loading screen, then a FAKE system-error dialog
   (modern teasing, not old-fashioned).
 - Walks through a runaway button, a fake/empty gift, a dreamy reveal,
   an Explorer "collect the props" game, compliment cards, a scrapbook
   of memories, a love letter, a blow-the-candles cake, a confetti finale,
   and a circular 'the end?' bonus ending.
 - A hidden easter egg: click the "mujhe mat dabao" button 5+ times to
   unlock a SECRET REWARD overlay.
 - Uses real animated stickers everywhere: a teddy, "pookie" stickers,
   and emoji webm stickers, all rendered as inline <video>/<img>.

Vibe: modern glassmorphism + animated gradient background + soft candy
colors (rose #f76a99, lavender #b79cff, sky #6db5ff) + handwritten font.

Tone of ALL text: playful Hinglish, affectionate, teasing, flirty-but-
respectful, and spoken in the formal-romantic "aap" register.

He treats this site as a love letter + roast + party all in one: the copy
roasts her gently AND flatters her hard.

4. HOW THEY TALK  (the vibe / register)
================================================================================
 - REGISTER: Formal-romantic "aap" (aapne/aapke/aapki). NOT "tu".
   Feminine verb endings: "aap karti hain", "aap hoti hain", "jeet gayi aap".
 - LANGUAGE: Hinglish (Hindi words in English script, mixed with English).
 - NICKNAMES: used ONE at a time, placed where they fit:
     mommy → teasing/nagging/couple jokes
     dora  → soft, affectionate moments
     baccha → fond, little-one moments ("baccha toh ho hi aap")
   NEVER all together in one line (that was an earlier mistake, fixed).
 - TEASING: playful roasting that always flips into a compliment.
 - FLIRT: blush-worthy, light-naughty but classy pickup lines — NOT the
   generic "r/seducingstrangers" cringe. Lines are warm and specific.

Example signature lines:
 - Compliment: "mommy, aap normal din bhi latka festival bana deti hain.
   party aap ho — baaki sab toh bas log hain"
 - Flirt: "aap hasaati hain aur mera dimag full system-shutdown ho jaata

 - Tease: "jeet gayi aap. ab hamesha jeetna, main kya karu"
 - Secret line: "innocent lagti hain, par aapko lagta hai main woh nahi

5. THE FULL SCENE-BY-SCENE FLOW
================================================================================
All scene wiring lives in src/App.jsx. Index constants:
  LOADING=0  FAKE_ERROR=1  WELCOME=2  RUN_AWAY=3  FAKE_GIFT=4  DREAM=5
  cat1=6  EXPLORE=7  COMPLIMENTS=8  SCRAPBOOK=9  cat2=10  LETTER=11
  cat3=12  CAKE=13  FINALE=14  FAKE_END=15  TRUE_END=16

 0  FakeLoading   — fake progress bar ("Compiling birthday.exe", "load kar
                    raha hoon… bas ek second bharosa rakhiye").
 1  FakeErrorScene — fake SYSTEM ERROR dialogs that "fix" into a compliment
                    ("system aapke bina chalta hi nahi").
 2  Welcome       — "hey Sneha, aaja!! 👋" + wave teddy + "kuch banaya hai
                    aapke liye ✨" + "shuru karte hain".
 3  RunawayScene  — a "continue →" button that RUNS AWAY from the cursor;
                    teasing taunts on each dodge, then it surrenders.
 4  FakeGiftScene — tap the gift → it opens → EMPTY?! 😦 → "RUK JAA!! asli
                    gift ko dramatic entry chahiye thi".
 5  DreamReveal   — confetti + "welcome to AAPKA din 🎉" + pookie sticker row
                    + "explore karein".
 6  cat1          — "cute" cat lottie break.
 7  ExplorerScene — 8 collectible props (star, flower, cloud, sparkle,
                    balloon, note, moon, hidden heart). When all 8 found →
                    shows a random pickup line + "sab mil gaye! mast 🔥".
                    (FIXED: teddy used to overlap the 8th "heart" so it
                     couldn't be clicked; teddy is now pointer-events:none.)
 8  ComplimentDeck— flip cards "kyun special hain aap 💝" — 10 reasons incl.
                    mommy teases + flirty lines, with a CutenessMeter.
 9  ScrapbookScene— polaroid memories ("humari scrapbook 📔").
10  cat2          — "smug" cat break.
11  LetterScene   — "aapke naam ek chitthi" — typewriter love letter, ends
                    "— aapka apna, sirf aapka 🥺" + "ps: mommy, aaj aap bas
                    party karo. baaki main sambhalunga. 💙".
12  cat3          — "sleepy" cat break.
13  CakeScene     — 3 candles; blow via microphone OR hold-button; "wish ho
                    gayi 🌠".
14  FinaleScene   — "HAPPY BIRTHDAY" letter burst + confetti + dance teddy.
                    bonus variant: "…AUR EK AUR CHEEZ!!".
15  FinalMessage  — "khatam? 🤔" button. First press → bonus finale (go 14);
                    after bonus, next press → TrueEnd (16). (circular flow)
16  TrueEnd       — "ab hum sach me done hain 💖" + "mommy, ek aur baar —
                    happy birthday. aap meri best cheez ho" + replay.

EASTER EGG: the floating "mujhe mat dabao" button in the HUD. Click 5+
times → SECRET_REWARD overlay ("SECRET UNLOCKED 🔓" + love teddy).

6. ALL THE COPY  (source of truth)
================================================================================
Every line of text lives in ONE file:
    src\utils\content.js
Edit there and the whole site updates. Sections:
    GIRL, NICKNAMES,
    FAKE_LOADING_STEPS, FAKE_ERRORS, FAKE_ERROR_FIX,
    TAUNTS, COMPLIMENTS, PICKUP_LINES, COMPLIMENT_GRADIENTS,
    MEMORIES, LETTER_LINES, CAT_BREAKS, EXPLORER_PROPS,
    FINALE_LETTERS, FINAL_MESSAGE, SECRET_REWARD.
A few short strings are hardcoded inside scene files (e.g. button labels),
but 95% of copy is centralized in content.js.

================================================================================
8. HOW TO EDIT & REBUILD
================================================================================
 - Edit text   : open src\utils\content.js, save.
 - Swap stickers: edit src\utils\assets.js path maps, or replace file in
                  public\assets\...
 - Rebuild     : in the birthday-site folder run:  npm run build
 - The live server reads /dist on every request (no-store), so after a
   build just hard-refresh the browser (Ctrl+F5) at http://localhost:4173
